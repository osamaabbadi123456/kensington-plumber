- Seven service detail pages are implemented: Emergency Plumber, Leak Repair, Blocked Toilet, Toilet Repair, Tap Repair, Bathroom Plumbing and Shower Repair.

## Completed foundations

- Canonical metadata, `robots.ts`, `sitemap.ts` and safe structured data are implemented.
- Netlify deployments are source-configured for `noindex, nofollow` and disallow-all `robots.txt` until the owner-controlled `NETLIFY_PUBLIC_INDEXING=true` launch variable is explicitly enabled. Canonicals remain on the public domain.
- The footer has named Privacy, Terms and Cookies links, and the legal routes are in the sitemap, route audit and smoke suite.
- Source QA covers contamination, business contact consistency, internal links, SEO route inventory, lint, TypeScript and production build.
- Final local smoke result: 76 Chromium tests passed with 4 intentional cross-viewport skips, covering all 31 canonical routes, controlled area-prefilled enquiry actions, routing anchors, legal routes and the reduced-motion safeguard at desktop and 390px mobile. The local smoke wrapper uses dedicated port 3401 and closes only its own production-server child after the run.
- Visual QA passed for 17 requested routes at 1440px, 768px and 390px, including the six confirmed-area pages, homepage, hubs and relevant service routes.
- A local Lighthouse baseline is recorded: mobile 96 Performance / 100 Accessibility / 100 Best Practices / 100 SEO; desktop 100 in all four categories. It used temporary Lighthouse tooling and a local production server; see `reports/lighthouse/summary.md`.
- Services, Problems and Areas now make destination type clear: `View service`/`View problem` for dedicated pages, `Read guidance` for existing guidance and `Build enquiry` for the guided contact path.
- Route, content-similarity, performance-source and image-plan reviews are recorded in `reports/` and `docs/IMAGE_PLAN.md`.
- The reviewed duplicate `kensington_codex_bootstrap/` folder was removed; active root source, documentation, scripts and tests were preserved.

## Known limitations

- The local Lighthouse baseline is not a deployed-host measurement. Preview and production Lighthouse checks remain separate.
- Browser smoke tests do not send WhatsApp messages or email, and are not a full WCAG audit or cross-browser suite.
- Production host behaviour, redirects and deployed asset delivery remain unverified.
- The owner-only WhatsApp/email/device and mailbox checks remain OWNER PENDING in `reports/OWNER_REAL_DEVICE_TEST.md`.

## Release and rollback

Production is **not authorised**. After explicit owner approval, commit the reviewed local changes, push `main`, verify CI, update and test the Netlify preview, obtain separate production approval, deploy, validate `/robots.txt`, `/sitemap.xml`, canonicals, favicon, Open Graph image, legal pages and contact routes, then connect Search Console and Bing, submit the sitemap once and capture a Day-0 baseline. Retain a recoverable previous version and a rollback path before publication. Do not deploy, push, publish, or change DNS as part of this repository state.
