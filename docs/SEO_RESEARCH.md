## Cannibalisation risks

- Homepage vs Areas: homepage owns plumber Kensington; Areas explains location scope.
- Emergency vs leak/burst pipe: emergency owns urgency; leak repair owns fault and damage limitation.
- Blocked toilet vs toilet repair: blockage/rising water versus running, flush, cistern and repair/replacement faults.
- Bathroom plumbing vs tap/shower/toilet: bathroom is room-level; fixtures own specific faults.
- Radiator/central heating vs boiler repair: water-based radiator symptoms must not imply gas capability.
- Water pressure vs tap/no-water: choose one canonical owner per query.
- Commercial vs domestic: keep audience and building context distinct.
