## Contact and routing model

- WhatsApp and email are public enquiry channels, not a promise of appointment, dispatch, availability or response time.
- The public contact flow may say that an enquiry can be routed to a plumbing professional covering a confirmed area. It must not state that a professional is definitely available or that a visit is confirmed.
- A useful enquiry can include a confirmed area or relevant postcode, affected room or fixture, visible symptom, when it started and a photo if it is safe and useful.

## Publication guardrails

Do not publish any of the following without new, documented evidence from the owner:

- A physical or trading address
- Reviews, ratings, testimonials, team members, or job counts
- Years of experience
- Accreditations, certifications, insurance, or Gas Safe claims
- Prices, fees, discounts, or payment terms
- Response times, same-day service, emergency attendance, or 24/7 availability
- Guarantees or warranties
- Exact service coverage beyond Kensington/W8, South Kensington, West Kensington, Earl's Court, Notting Hill, North Kensington and Holland Park

Radiator or heating wording may invite a plumbing enquiry about a visible radiator, valve or pipework concern only. It must not imply boiler diagnosis, gas appliance work, Gas Safe registration, certification, availability or a completed service.

Do not describe any area outside Kensington/W8, South Kensington, West Kensington, Earl's Court, Notting Hill, North Kensington and Holland Park as covered unless the owner records explicit confirmation here.

Copy must describe an enquiry pathway and must not imply that the website itself is a plumbing company or that a particular contractor will accept or complete a job.
