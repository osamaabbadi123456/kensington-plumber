# Plumbing glossary plan

Status: research foundation only. No public glossary route exists yet.

Purpose: prepare short, natural British-English explanations of terms a visitor may see in an enquiry, without turning the site into DIY instructions or publishing a route before the content is reviewed.

| Term | Plain-English meaning | Where a homeowner may encounter it | Relevant current route |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stopcock / stop tap | The main valve that controls the water supply into a property. | Near the point where the water supply enters, sometimes under a sink, in a cupboard or by a meter. | [Leak repair](/services/leak-repair), [ceiling-water advice](/plumbing-advice/what-to-do-if-water-is-coming-through-your-ceiling) |
| Isolation valve | A small valve that can shut off water to one fitting or appliance. | On pipework serving a toilet, tap, washing machine or similar fixture. | [Toilet repair](/services/toilet-repair), [tap repair](/services/tap-repair) |
| Cistern | The tank that stores water for a toilet flush. | Behind, above or inside the toilet. | [Toilet repair](/services/toilet-repair), [toilet keeps running](/plumbing-problems/toilet-keeps-running) |
| Trap | A curved section of waste pipe that holds water to help prevent smells coming back into the room. | Under a basin, sink, bath or shower waste. | [Bathroom plumbing](/services/bathroom-plumbing), [leak repair](/services/leak-repair) |
| Waste pipe | Pipework that carries used water away from a basin, bath, shower or appliance. | Below sinks, baths, showers and appliance connections. | [Bathroom plumbing](/services/bathroom-plumbing), [blocked toilet](/services/blocked-toilet) |
| Flexi hose | A flexible connector between pipework and a fitting or appliance. | Below taps, toilets and washing-machine connections. | [Leak repair](/services/leak-repair), [tap repair](/services/tap-repair) |
| Compression fitting | A pipe connector tightened around a pipe with a nut and ring. | On visible copper or plastic pipework, often near valves and repairs. | [Leak repair](/services/leak-repair) |
| Mixer tap | A tap that mixes hot and cold water through one spout or control. | Kitchen and bathroom basin taps, and some baths. | [Tap repair](/services/tap-repair), [bathroom plumbing](/services/bathroom-plumbing) |
| Ball valve / fill valve | A cistern part that controls water refilling after a flush. | Inside a toilet cistern. | [Toilet repair](/services/toilet-repair), [toilet keeps running](/plumbing-problems/toilet-keeps-running) |
| Soil pipe | The larger pipe carrying toilet waste away from the property. | Behind a toilet or on an external wall. | [Blocked toilet](/services/blocked-toilet) |
| Overflow | A route for excess water if a cistern or tank keeps filling beyond its normal level. | At a toilet cistern, tank or external overflow pipe. | [Toilet keeps running](/plumbing-problems/toilet-keeps-running), [leak repair](/services/leak-repair) |
| Radiator valve | A valve that controls water flow into or out of a radiator. | At the lower side of a radiator. | [Services hub](/services) |
| TRV | Thermostatic radiator valve: a radiator control with a temperature setting. | On one side of many radiators. | [Services hub](/services) |

## Editorial rules for a later glossary

- Explain a term before using it and use British wording where there is a common UK form, such as “stop tap”.
- Link only to a current relevant route; do not create term pages or new service claims simply to complete the list.
- Keep safety language descriptive. Do not publish risky DIY repair sequences.
- Review each definition and its linked route before making a glossary indexable.
