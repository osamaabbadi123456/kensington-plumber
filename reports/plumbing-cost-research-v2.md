# Plumbing cost research V2 — exact public figures

Status: internal research only. Checked 2026-08-26. This report records figures publicly displayed by third-party sources; it is not a price list, quote, rate card or public-facing claim by Plumber Kensington. No figure here has been published in the website UI, metadata or schema.

## Method and interpretation

- Geographic scope is recorded exactly as stated or inferred from the source context. Kensington is not assumed to match a UK or London-wide figure.
- “Unclear” means the page did not make that inclusion explicit for that figure. It does not mean the item is included.
- Checkatrade figures labelled “Cost + VAT” are recorded as VAT included in the listed range. Its hourly/call-out tables do not consistently state VAT treatment, so those rows stay unclear unless a source says otherwise.
- A provider or marketplace guide is evidence of that publisher’s stated guide/rate, not proof of a market average. London provider-style figures are kept separate from UK editorial guides.
- No half-hour figure has been converted to an hourly figure. No calculated figure is used below.

## Source-quality observations and disagreement log

- Checkatrade and MyBuilder are separate editorial/marketplace cost-guide publishers. Their common UK hourly (£40–£60) and day-rate (£325–£375) figures align, but that agreement does not make them a Kensington rate.
- Checkatrade’s emergency call-out range is £100–£120, while MyBuilder’s is £50–£150. Their scopes/inclusions are not identical; do not merge them into a claimed public “average”.
- The London guide from Energy North is useful as a London-context comparator but identifies itself as a marketplace guide, not an independent survey. The MyBuilder radiator guide provides a job-specific London £90/hour labour statement, not a general rate card.
- The Greater London tap-repair listing is a directory/listing result and has weaker methodology than an editorial guide. It should not control a public range.
- A single-source number is retained as research evidence, not elevated into a recommendation. This particularly applies to indoor tap replacement, toilet replacement, blocked-toilet jobs, and radiator valve work.

## Decision

No public price guide, price card, pricing schema or price CTA should be created from this research. An owner would need to approve a separate editorial scope, source policy, disclaimers and a review cadence before any price information is considered for publication.
