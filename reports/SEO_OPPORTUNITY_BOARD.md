## Reading the board

- **Highest scores prefer existing ownership.** They are deliberately not a quota of future URLs.
- **“After design approval” is not permission to generate or source images.** It requires the asset/rights process in the image plans.
- **“After GSC data” means after Search Console has gathered enough route/query impressions to verify a distinct cluster**, not immediately after the property is connected.
- **“Do not do” rows are useful controls**, not missed opportunities.
