# Plumbing cost research — internal only

Checked: 2026-08-25. This is market research for a later, owner-approved price-guide decision. It is not public site copy, a quotation, a promise of price, or evidence of this website's rates or coverage.

## Research method and limits

- Prioritised published UK trade-cost guides, then London-specific public rate pages as context.
- Kensington-specific price data was not verified. London figures are not assumed to apply to Kensington or to a routed professional.
- A source may include VAT, exclude VAT, include a call-out, or use a different job scope. Those differences prevent direct comparison.
- No cost figures have been added to public pages, structured data, metadata or enquiry messages.

## Findings by research topic

| Topic | Evidence found | Interpretation for future research |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard hourly rates | Checkatrade's UK guides show a national labour range, while its London landing page shows higher London hourly context. Aspect's public London provider rate card is higher again once its half-hour rate is doubled. | The sources conflict because their scopes, inclusion rules and commercial models differ. Do not publish one “London hourly rate” without explaining scope and quotation variables. |
| Call-out charges | Checkatrade distinguishes normal hourly work from emergency call-outs; its source says emergency call-outs are usually additional and parts can be extra. | A future guide should ask users to confirm call-out amount, first-hour inclusion, labour rate, VAT and parts before work. |
| Emergency plumbing | Checkatrade's London emergency landing page gives a public London estimate range, while national guides explain that out-of-hours work can cost more. | Research supports explaining why urgent work may be priced differently, but does not support any Plumber Kensington price or availability claim. |
| Blocked toilet | Checkatrade's toilet-unblocking guide provides a UK materials-and-labour range with the call-out described as included for the stated job type. | Useful for a later UK-only guide with clear caveats. External drainage, jetting, CCTV or out-of-hours work are not comparable to a simple blockage. |
| Leak / pipe repair | Checkatrade provides accessible pipe-repair and water-supply-pipe context, and explicitly flags access and reinstatement as major variables. | A later guide must separate a visible fitting leak from hidden-pipe locating, ceiling opening and making-good work. |
| Tap repair / replacement | Checkatrade's water-pipe guide includes a leaking-tap repair figure, but the reviewed sources did not give a sufficiently comparable current London replacement figure. | Keep tap replacement research open; do not infer a replacement price from a leaking-tap repair. |
| Toilet repair / replacement | The reviewed sources provide blockage and general plumber-cost context, but not a sufficiently comparable current London toilet cistern/flush repair or replacement benchmark. | Treat toilet repair/replacement as a separate future research question. |
| Radiator plumbing | Checkatrade's common-job table includes radiator installation rather than a broadly comparable repair figure. | Do not turn installation pricing into radiator-repair guidance; repair, valve work and system work need separate evidence. |

## Price-bearing observations kept internal

The current research includes several price-bearing source statements, but this report deliberately does not consolidate them into a recommended public range. The sources differ on location, whether VAT is included, whether a call-out/first hour is included, whether parts are included, access difficulty, urgency and job definition.

## Conflict and disclosure notes

- The UK cost-guide figures should not be presented as Kensington/London prices.
- The London provider rate card is one provider's stated rate, not market-wide evidence.
- The Checkatrade London landing page is a directory/search context, not a verified Kensington sample.
- Emergency, gas/boiler and provider availability claims from third-party pages are not adopted by this project.
- Any future public guide needs an owner-approved editorial scope, date-reviewed policy, source citations, clear “not a quote” language and a separate final fact review.

## Decision

Pricing research remains unpublished. No price page, price structured data, price CTA or price claim is justified by this batch.
