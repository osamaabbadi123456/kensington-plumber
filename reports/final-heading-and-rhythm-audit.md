# Final heading and page-rhythm audit

Checked locally: 2026-08-28. Scope: homepage, hubs, all current service and problem pages, and six area pages.

## Heading result

- Every current public route has one meaningful H1; the source SEO audit and browser smoke suite enforce this route-wide.
- H2 elements label major decision, guidance, related-route and contact sections. H3 elements are confined to card groups, choices and sub-points within those sections.
- Area pages keep a shared structural hierarchy for predictability, but their H1 context, postcode treatment, local decision heading and selected routes are distinct.
- No heading was added solely as a styling device, and no keyword-only heading was introduced.

## Rhythm result

- Homepage order is now: problem → matching service → clear details/send route → confirmed areas → reviewed guidance/resources → contact path. This makes the symptom the first decision and prevents service cards from dominating the journey.
- The homepage retains its approved lightweight visual and uses a server-rendered route to the full Contact builder instead of duplicating the large interactive form in the initial page bundle.
- The Services hub now uses an actual radiator/heating service card rather than a same-page anchor. The remaining short depth cards cover only pipework and basin/sink guidance.
- Area pages use a compact postcode/covered-area identity strip, one local decision section, four relevant routes, useful details, CTA and nearby-area links. This reduces card density without creating location-template pages.

## Internal-link result

- Every key commercial route has a relevant visible route and an enquiry action.
- The new radiator page links to Leak Repair, the glossary and enquiry-detail advice; it does not link to boiler or gas content.
- Area pages contain four service/problem destinations each, plus limited nearby confirmed-area links. No all-area footer expansion or keyword-anchor repetition was added.
