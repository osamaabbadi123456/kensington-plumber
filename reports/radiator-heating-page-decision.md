# Radiator and heating page decision

Checked: 2026-08-28. Live SERP review covered `radiator repair Kensington`, `radiator plumber Kensington`, `radiator leak Kensington` and `central heating plumber Kensington`.

## Result: Option A — build one bounded page

`/services/radiator-heating` is justified as a single indexable route because the search results and Kensington marketplace listings repeatedly recognise radiator repair, radiator valves and radiator plumbing as distinct visitor needs. The page consolidates those closely related non-gas symptoms instead of creating separate valve, leak, cold-spot or central-heating URLs.

## Evidence and boundary

| Finding | Implication |
| --- | --- |
| Results include radiator-repair and radiator-valve categories for Kensington, alongside independent radiator repair pages. | The visitor intent is distinct enough from generic Leak Repair and Tap Repair. |
| `central heating plumber Kensington` results are heavily dominated by boiler, gas-engineer, installation, certification, price and availability claims. | Do not create a broad boiler/heating page or make gas, boiler, certification, attendance, price or availability claims. |
| Owner-confirmed scope includes radiator / central-heating plumbing work. | A page can cover radiator leaks, visible valves, pipework and user-observable uneven-warmth symptoms. |

## Implemented intent guardrails

- Owns: radiator leaks, visible valves or pipework, one-versus-several radiator symptoms, cold/uneven areas as an observation, and useful photos/details.
- Does not own: boiler diagnosis or repair, gas work, boiler installation, combustion, certification, Gas Safe status, prices or response-time promises.
- Links to Leak Repair, the reviewed glossary and the enquiry-details guide; it does not create a boiler funnel.
- The Services hub and homepage now send radiator visitors to this substantive page rather than an in-page anchor.

## Source context

The live result set contained independent plumbing/heating sites plus directories such as Checkatrade. It was used to assess intent only; no competitor copy, review, price, Gas Safe, emergency or availability claim was copied.
