# Area-page uniqueness audit

Checked locally: 2026-08-27, after the final local-area targeting reset.

## Shared structure, unique inputs

All six area pages intentionally use the same sequence: local introduction/postcode context, local enquiry angle, selected services, selected symptoms, four useful details, transparent enquiry action and nearby confirmed areas. A visitor should not need to relearn the layout between locations.

| Page | Unique local angle | Distinct selected route mix | Unique location detail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| South Kensington | Start with room and fixture. | Leak Repair, Bathroom Plumbing, Toilet Repair; ceiling-water and tap routes. | SW7 context and room/fixture change prompts. |
| West Kensington | Separate pipework from toilet symptoms. | Leak Repair pipework, Blocked Toilet, Bathroom Plumbing; flush and low-flow guidance. | W14 and one-fixture-versus-several prompt. |
| Earl's Court | Describe where water appears first. | Leak Repair, Emergency Plumbing, Blocked Toilet; ceiling symptom and safety advice. | SW5 and room-above/below prompt. |
| Notting Hill | Make the visible fitting the starting point. | Tap Repair, Bathroom Plumbing, Shower Repair; shower symptom and Leak Repair. | W11 and close/wide fitting-photo prompt. |
| North Kensington | Choose the clearest visible symptom. | Toilet Repair, Tap Repair, Leak Repair; running and non-flushing toilet routes. | W10 and fixture-versus-pipework prompt. |
| Holland Park | Use the actual postcode to keep location clear. | Leak Repair, Shower Repair, Bathroom Plumbing; running-toilet and low-flow guidance. | Explicit W8/W11 boundary and start-time prompt. |

## Result

The pages share only intentional navigation, transparent routing language and CTA architecture. Their local introductions, headings, postcode wording, enquiry prompts and existing route selections are not copied blindly. No page claims local jobs, premises, teams, reviews, availability or a service outcome.
