# Earned-link opportunity research

Status: research only. Checked 2026-08-27. No account was created and no person/organisation was contacted.

## Scope and ethics

Plumber Kensington is an enquiry / lead-generation website, not a verified physical plumbing company. A legitimate opportunity must never require a false address, engineer identity, Gas Safe registration, completed job, review, customer history, paid link or an invented local presence. Many relevant organisations below are **citation sources, not outreach targets**—they are useful because future safety/terminology content can cite them, not because they are likely to link back.

“Prospect” therefore means a researched potential relationship or reference category, not an entitlement to a link. Every action needs manual eligibility, editorial-policy and truth-pack checks first.

## Two realistic earned-link paths, conditional on proof

1. **A genuinely useful neutral resource.** After launch, publish no promotional page until owner approval; then an evidence-led “what details help identify a water leak / toilet fault” resource could be offered to a relevant property-maintenance publisher *only if* it accepts non-affiliated educational contributions. The pitch must state the lead-generation model plainly and include no unverified service claims.
2. **Truthful directory eligibility.** Assess directory profiles only after the owner can document the operating entity, suitable contact details, permitted category and any required address/service proof. A listing is not a shortcut around the truth pack and must not collect or invent reviews.

## Explicitly excluded

- Google Business Profile, Bing Places and map/local-pack tactics without proof of eligibility and a truthful physical/service business representation.
- WaterSafe, Checkatrade, Yell, MyBuilder or trade-association profiles that would require a false trader, address, membership, certification or customer history.
- Paid guest posts, reciprocal-link schemes, coupon pages, “top plumber” lists, fabricated press releases, manufactured job photos, review solicitation and all paid-link arrangements.
- Any contact with the RBKC housing organisations in relation to sensitive tenant/repair matters or historical housing-safety events.

## Next safe action

Do not outreach yet. First obtain owner confirmation of the legal/operating entity and which factual proof, if any, may be used in a third-party profile. Until then, use the authority list to improve future source quality, not to pursue links.
