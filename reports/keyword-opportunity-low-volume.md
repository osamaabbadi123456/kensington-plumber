## Explicitly excluded from the roadmap

- 24-hour, same-day and response-time terms: they would require unsupported availability claims.
- Boiler, gas, radiator and broad heating terms: the public service truth does not support them.
- Price and call-out queries: internal cost research does not authorise public pricing.
- A standalone W8 page: the homepage already owns the confirmed Kensington/W8 intent; no evidence here justifies a duplicative postcode URL.
